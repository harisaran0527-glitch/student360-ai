# Student360 AI — Evaluator Demonstration Sequence (5–10 Minutes)

This presentation guide provides a structured walkthrough for institutional leaders, faculty advisors, and technical evaluators demonstrating the end-to-end capabilities of **Student360 AI**.

---

## Demo Sequence Overview

### Step 1: Admin Login & Executive Analytics Hub (2 Minutes)
1. Navigate to `/login` and sign in as Super Admin (`admin@student360.edu` / `Admin@1234`).
2. Explore the **Executive Analytics Hub** (`/admin/analytics`).
3. Point out real database metrics: Total Students, Attendance Average %, Placement Rate %, Verification Backlog, and Rule-Based Support Attention summary.
4. Demonstrate interactive chart drill-downs (click a metric card to navigate to filtered student lists).

### Step 2: Central Admin Reporting Center & Multi-Format Exports (2 Minutes)
1. Open **Central Reports Center** (`/admin/reports`).
2. Select report categories (Student Master, Attendance Shortage, Internship Compliance, Placement Pipeline).
3. Apply filters (Department, Batch, Semester).
4. Demonstrate **CSV Export**, **Excel (.xlsx) Export** with formatted headers, and **Print-Friendly PDF View** (`@media print`).
5. Show saved report filter presets and scheduled report configurations.

### Step 3: Faculty Attendance Marking & Scope Verification (2 Minutes)
1. Log in as Faculty (`faculty@student360.edu` / `Faculty@1234`).
2. Open **Mark Attendance** (`/faculty/attendance`).
3. Demonstrate quick "Mark All Present", toggle individual attendance statuses (Present, Absent, OD, Late), and save session.
4. Show **Verification Queue** (`/faculty/verification`) to approve student certificates and project showcases.
5. Highlight that faculty access is restricted server-side to assigned department scope.

### Step 4: Student 360° Profile & AI Career Center (2 Minutes)
1. Log in as Student (`student@student360.edu` / `Student@1234`).
2. View **Master Digital Profile** (`/student/profile`) and **Skill Matrix & Passport** (`/student/skills`).
3. Show strict separation of Verified Skills (star badge) vs Self-Reported Skills.
4. Open **AI Career Center** (`/student/ai-career-center`).
5. Highlight the **Rule-Based Engine v1.0** transparency box, "Why am I seeing this?" breakdowns, evidence percentages, and 5-phase personalized learning roadmap.

### Step 5: Installable PWA & Offline Diagnostics (1 Minute)
1. Open **PWA & Mobile Diagnostics** (`/admin/pwa-health`).
2. Show active Service Worker status (`/sw.js`), Web App Manifest detection, standalone mode, and offline fallback strategy (`/offline.html`).
3. Demonstrate mobile responsiveness by shrinking browser viewport to mobile dimensions.
