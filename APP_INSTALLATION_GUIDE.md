# Student360 AI — Application Installation Guide

## 1. Android Chrome Installation

1. Open Student360 AI URL in Google Chrome.
2. Click the **"Install Student360 AI App"** prompt banner or open the Chrome menu (⋮) and tap **"Install App"**.
3. Confirm installation. The Student360 AI app icon will be added to your home screen and launcher.

---

## 2. iOS Safari Add to Home Screen

1. Open Student360 AI URL in Apple Safari on iOS.
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Confirm app title "Student360" and tap **Add**.

---

## 3. Desktop Installation (Chrome / Edge)

1. Open Student360 AI in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the **Install** icon in the address bar right corner.
3. Launch Student360 AI in standalone windowed mode.
